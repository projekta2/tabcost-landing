❓ Frequently Asked Questions
💰 How is cost calculated?
(Hourly rate / 60) × impact % (default 20%). Example: $50/h → $0.166 per inactive minute per tab. Adjustable in Pro.
🔒 Are my data sent to a server?
Never. All data stays in your browser's local storage. Privacy absolute.
📎 How to export inactive tabs to CSV? (Pro)
Open options panel → Critical Tabs Audit → blue "Export inactive tabs to CSV". Generates title, URL, minutes, cost.
🔄 Can I reset daily cost early?
Yes, Pro version includes a reset button (↺) in the popup.
