🚀 Welcome guide & Pro activation

Your license key is unique. Keep it safe – also delivered in your Gumroad confirmation email. Follow these steps to unlock TabCost Pro:

  1. Install the free extension from Chrome Web Store (search "TabCost").
  2. Click the TabCost icon in your toolbar → open the popup panel.
  3. Set your hourly rate (e.g., $50/h or €50/h) so the real opportunity cost is shown.
  4. Click "Unlock Premium" (in popup footer). A license input appears.
  5. Paste your license key XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX exactly as in your Gumroad email → press Activate.
  6. Your badge turns to PRO instantly. All advanced features are unlocked — no restart needed.

⭐ What you've unlocked: 365-day history, auto‑close tabs, smart notifications, CSV export, ignored domains, adjustable grace minutes (1‑30) and impact % (10‑50%).

💡 Tip: If activation fails, copy the key without extra spaces. Contact hello@projekta2.com for help.

📌 What is TabCost?

TabCost turns your inactive tabs into a real economic metric. It's not just a tab manager: it's an attention auditor that assigns a monetary value to the time you lose.

  • ✅ Become aware of how much money "slips away" with forgotten tabs.
  • ✅ Automatically close what you don't use (Pro).
  • ✅ Export inactive tabs to CSV (Pro) to analyze habits.

🎯 Who is it for?

  • Freelancers charging hourly to minimize distractions.
  • Students & professionals needing concentration.
  • Remote teams improving productivity.

⚙️ How is the cost calculated?

Cost per minute = (Hourly rate ÷ 60) × Impact percentage

  • Default impact 20% (adjustable Pro). Active tab never generates cost.
  • Example: $50/h → ~$0.166/min per inactive tab.
